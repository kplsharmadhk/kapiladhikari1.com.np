01 / THE PERSON

A JOURNEY ACROSS
MEDIA & TECHNOLOGY.

A professional path shaped by journalism, editorial work, digital media and a growing focus on software and app development.

Active in Nepali journalism from an early age, Kapil Sharma Adhikari's work has included reporting, desk editing, news presentation and digital media.

His editorial interests include Nepali politics, current affairs, entertainment and defence matters. Alongside newsroom work, he has turned toward software and app development — exploring how technology can transform ideas into practical digital products.

“Media tells the stories. Technology creates new ways to experience and share them.”
