## Modifications in this website
The website loads the two prebuilt GLB files and their catalog from the versioned jsDelivr npm package. The viewer, layout, Nepali interface, selection behavior, category controls, simple motion effect and quiz are project-side adaptations. The model geometry itself is not claimed as original LokSewaX work.

## Scope and safety
The models are for education and visualization only. They are not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment or clinical decisions. The male and female references have different source composition and coverage. The model catalog groups some bones and muscles; it does not provide a separately selectable mesh for every one of the 206 adult bones.
