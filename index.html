01 — ABOUT ME

Born in the hills.
Drawn to the horizon.

01 Arghakhanchi, Nepal

I am Kapil Adhikari, a Nepali traveler from the beautiful hills of Arghakhanchi.

I travel to discover the places that don't always appear on a map — quiet mountain trails, small villages, local cultures, hidden viewpoints and the stories waiting along the road.

For me, traveling is not simply about reaching a destination. It is about the people I meet, the roads I take and the memories created along the way.
