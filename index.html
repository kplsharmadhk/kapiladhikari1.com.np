01 — ABOUT

From the newsroom. Toward the next horizon.

KAPIL SHARMA ADHIKARI ARghakhanchi · NEPAL

Saanai umersdekhi Nepali patrakaritama sakriya, Kapil Sharma Adhikari's journey has grown from journalism and editorial work into technology and digital product development.

He has worked across reporting, desk editing, news presentation and digital media, with particular interest in Nepali politics, current affairs, entertainment and defence matters.

Alongside journalism, he is now focused on software and app development — exploring how ideas can be transformed into useful digital products and experiences.

His work sits at an intersection of media, technology and exploration — three areas that continue to shape the way he learns, works and creates.
